# Roadmap

The next phase is about evidence and accessibility rather than adding more
features.

- Test language and defaults with neurodivergent users.
- Add controls for step size, time visibility, nudge cadence and reduced
  stimulation.
- Bring the browser experience onto the shared TypeScript engine.
- Add import, export and delete controls for any persistent local data.
- Expand synthetic behavioural scenarios for overwhelm, interruption, drift,
  abandonment and return.
- Document local-model adapters and threat boundaries.

Requests are welcome, but the core should stay small enough to understand.
