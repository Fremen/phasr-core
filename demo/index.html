Phasr is an experiment in calmer software for task initiation, sequencing, time awareness and returning after interruption.

This demonstration is deliberately deterministic. The full TypeScript engine adds nested sessions, local stores, five modes and optional model assistance.

Built in the open for people whose brains do not always follow the plan.
